04. Projects

FEATURED · LIVE ON GOOGLE PLAY

MitaWise — Expense & Income Tracker

A personal-finance Android app I designed, built and published on my own. Users track expenses and income, see monthly insights, and manage recurring payments like EMIs and rent.

  • Offline-first: re-architected from a server-backed app to on-device storage, with atomic writes and separate data for each account on the same phone.
  • Google Drive backup: backup, restore and silent daily auto-backup to the user's private Drive app folder, using OAuth and the least-privilege drive.appdata scope.
  • Features: category and monthly analytics, running balance, calendar view, recurring expenses, tags, CSV/JSON export and smart reminders.
  • Full product: Node.js + PostgreSQL REST API, React admin dashboard, Firebase Analytics, AdMob, and Play Store release with EAS.
